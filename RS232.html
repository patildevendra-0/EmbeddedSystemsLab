UART vs RS-232
UART = framing/logic · RS-232 = electrical interface
UART defines how bits are framed (start/data/parity/stop) at 0–3.3V/5V TTL logic. RS-232 defines a separate electrical standard—bipolar ±voltage signaling—that a transceiver chip (e.g. a MAX232-class driver) converts UART logic into. A raw MCU UART pin should never be wired directly into an RS-232 port.
