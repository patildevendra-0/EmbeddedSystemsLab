Retained vs Live Delivery

A live message only reaches clients already subscribed at the moment it's published. A retained message is stored by the broker and handed to any client that subscribes later.

Last Will & Testament

Devices can register a will message. If they disconnect ungracefully, the broker publishes it on their behalf — useful for "device offline" alerts.
