Consecutive data packets on a pipe alternate DATA0/DATA1 so a receiver can detect and discard a duplicate retransmission, without needing sequence numbers.

Hub & Topology

USB is hierarchical, not flat: a root hub inside the host can connect directly to devices or to further hubs, which fan out to more devices.

Host
└─ Root Hub
   ├─ Keyboard (Addr 5)
   ├─ Hub (Addr 3)
   │   ├─ Mouse (Addr 6)
   │   └─ Flash Drive (Addr 7)
   └─ Camera (Addr 4)

USB-C & Power

USB-C is a connector/receptacle ecosystem — it does not by itself guarantee USB 3.x, USB4, Thunderbolt, or any particular power level. Those are negotiated separately.

CC Pins
Orientation + role detect
VBUS (default)
5V · up to 500mA/900mA
Power Delivery
Separate message protocol over CC
Not every USB-C device or cable supports USB Power Delivery, SuperSpeed data, or Alternate Modes — each is negotiated independently.
